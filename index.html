Banjma: Checkout

A 3D horror-adventure game for Mac.

Support

Questions, bugs or feedback? Email scottbhuff@gmail.com and include your macOS version and what happened.

  • Controls: W/S walk, A/D turn, Shift sprint, Space jump, E interact, V switch camera view, Esc pause and save. PlayStation and Xbox controllers also work.
  • Saves: your six save slots are stored on your Mac and load automatically.
  • Online co-op: sign in to Game Center in System Settings, then choose Play Online in the game.

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 24, 2026

Banjma: Checkout does not collect, store, transmit or sell any personal data.

  • Save slots, character outfits and progress are stored only on your Mac, in its Application Support folder.
  • Optional online co-op uses Apple Game Center (GameKit). Apple handles that connection under Apple's Privacy Policy.
  • The game has no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.
